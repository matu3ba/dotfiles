=uninstall
Based on https://argo-cd.readthedocs.io/en/latest/user-guide/app_deletion/
and https://oneuptime.com/blog/post/2026-02-26-uninstall-argocd-from-kubernetes/view

Option 1: Keep pods managed by ArgoCD running
>kubectl delete applications.argoproj.io,appprojects.argoproj.io --all -n argocd --cascade=orphan
Option 2: Kill pods manage by ArgoCD.
>{
kubectl delete applications.argoproj.io,appprojects.argoproj.io --all -n argocd --cascade=foreground
kubectl delete crd,clusterrole,clusterrolebinding -l app.kubernetes.io/part-of=argocd
kubectl delete namespace argocd
# force delete
kubectl get namespace argocd -o json | jq '.spec.finalizers = []' | kubectl replace --raw "/api/v1/namespaces/argocd/finalize" -f -
}<
