* when Windows containers not available
  * nix as contaier has best trade-offs
    - flexible handling of dockerfile + package selection
    - flexible handling of caching: handling local cache possible with container overlays or cache
    - simpler than mise with individual handling of configs
  * nix container to build selection of dev containers as OCI images
  * what mise does not have: config introspection, bumps, small image sizes, simple state, low ops overhead
  * what mise does have: simple guide with proper instructions
