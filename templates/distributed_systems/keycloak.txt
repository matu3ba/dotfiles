keycloak_login: {access_token,id_token,refresh_token,expires_in,refresh_expires_in}
* look for access_token und id_token which are for
  OAuth 2.0 (Authorization) und OpenID Connect (Authentication)
* shared between access_token and id_token: sub (Subject), sid (Session ID), auth_time (unique timepoint of user login),
* id_token: profile data
* access_token: permissions etc
* refresh_token: refresh the login after access_token invalidates after expires_in
* refresh_expires_in: user session expiry
* SSO Session Idle, SSO Session Max
